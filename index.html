Utsavam Activities ఉత్సవ కార్యక్రమాలు

Opening Day

Ganesh Sthapana & Maha Puja

Idol installation, Abhishekam, alankaram and Aarti.

Daily Seva

Devotional activities led by youth and families.

Maha Aarti & Visarjan

Concluding prayers, procession and prasadam.

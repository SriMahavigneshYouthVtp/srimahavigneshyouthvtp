Seven Days of Cultural Activities ఏడు రోజుల సాంస్కృతిక కార్యక్రమాలు

Every day brings a new opportunity for devotion, creativity and community fun.

01

Opening Puja & Kids Fun Games

Ganesh Puja followed by fun games and prizes for children.

02

Play by Youth

A lively stage play presented by local youth.

03

Dance Programme

Classical, folk and devotional dance performances.

04

Movie Projection

A family-friendly devotional or inspirational movie.

05

Music & Bhajan Evening

Group bhajans and musical performances.

06

Quiz, Drawing & Talent Day

Quiz, drawing, storytelling and talent showcase.

07

Grand Finale & Visarjan

Maha Aarti, prize distribution and farewell procession.

A meaningful celebration organised by SriMahavignesh Youth Association with devotion, transparency and togetherness.

Devotion & Seva

Daily pujas, bhajans and spiritual activities in the presence of Lord Ganesha.

🤝

Community Unity

Youth, elders and families come together to celebrate and serve the community.

Utsavam Activities ఉత్సవ కార్యక్రమాలు

Join us in prayer, celebration and service throughout the Utsavam.

01
Opening Day

Ganesh Sthapana & Maha Puja

Idol installation, Kalasha Sthapana, Maha Abhishekam, special alankaram and Aarti.

02
Daily Seva

Pujas, Bhajans & Annadanam

Morning and evening pujas, Sahasranama Archana, bhajans, cultural activities and prasadam.

03
Celebration

Cultural Programmes

Community performances and devotional activities led by our youth and local families.

04
Conclusion

Maha Aarti & Visarjan

Concluding Maha Aarti, procession and prasadam distribution with devotion.

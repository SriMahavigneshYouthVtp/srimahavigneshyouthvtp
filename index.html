About the Mahotsavam

SriMahavignesh Youth Association organises the annual Vinayaka Chavithi and Navaratri Mahotsavam with pure devotion. This platform maintains transparency of Chandas (collected funds), expenses and shares the joy of celebrations through photos and day-wise activities.

🙏

Devotion & Seva

Daily pujas, abhishekams, bhajans and special alankarams for Lord Ganesha and Goddess Durga.

🤝

Community Unity

Youth and elders come together for cultural programmes, annadanam and social service activities.

📊

Full Transparency

Every rupee of Chandas and every expense is recorded and displayed publicly for complete accountability.

📷

Memories Forever

Download high-quality photos of all activities by scanning the QR codes or visiting the gallery.

Day-by-Day Activities

Complete schedule of Vinayaka Chavithi & Navaratri celebrations. Timings may vary slightly – please check with organisers.

Day 1 – Vinayaka Chavithi

Ganesh Sthapana & Maha Puja

Morning: Ganesh idol installation, Kalasha Sthapana, Maha Abhishekam. Evening: Special Alankaram, Bhajans & Aarti.

Day 2 – 4

Daily Pujas & Cultural Programmes

Morning & Evening Abhishekam, Sahasranama Archana, Youth cultural events, Bhajan Sandhya and Annadanam.

Navaratri Days

Devi Alankaram & Special Homams

Daily different Alankarams of Goddess, Kumari Puja, Chandi Homam on special days, Garba & Dandiya evenings.

Final Day

Ganesh Visarjan & Maha Aarti

Grand procession, Visarjan of Lord Ganesha with full devotion, concluding Maha Aarti and prasadam distribution.
